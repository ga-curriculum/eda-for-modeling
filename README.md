## About
Explore data to inform modeling, including understand random variables and distributions, interpreting correlations, and standardizing data.

## Learning Objectives

*You will be able to:*

- Summarize the reasons and ways to explore data for the purpose of modeling and machine learning.
- Define a random variable in the context of statistics and data.
- Explain how identifying a variable's distribution helps inform our modelling approach.
- Interpret correlation as a measure of the strength of the linear relationship between variables.
- Explain the motivation behind and methods for standardizing data.
- Understand that correlation itself does not imply causation.
